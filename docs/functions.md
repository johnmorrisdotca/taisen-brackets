# Functions and options

What each family of functions is for, and the options of `drawBracket` and `roadToFinal`, in full. [API reference](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/taisen-brackets/docs/api/main/) on the documentation site is the list of every export with its signature, made from the source; the [guides](./guides/getting-started.md) show them at work.

## Every function

Everything is exported from `@johnmorrisdotca/taisen-brackets`; the layout part from `/layout`, the formats from `/presets`. The types are in the package; each function's doc comment has its details.

- `drawBracket(input, options?)`: the SVG string. `input` is a list of matchups, `{ matchups, teams }` or a layout. Options: `layout`, `headers`, `at`, `standings`, `qualify`, `style`, `styleScope`, `size`, `orientation`, `shape`, `detail`, `theme`, `background` (a preset's name, a tuned preset or your own picture), `vs`, `codes`, `title`, `subtitle`, `roundNames`, `roundLabels`, `scores`, `seeds`, `markShape`, `champion`, `trophy`, `emblems`, `emblemStyle`, `emblemTint`, `thirdPlace`, `winnerHighlight`, `look`, `href`, `tieClass`, `highlightPath`, `flags`, `flagHref`, `locale`, `words`, `teamName`, `groupOrder`, `id`, `className`.
- `roadToFinal(input, options?)`: the road to the final of a tournament or of two finalists: `{ options, title, summary, totals, draw(extra?), table(options?) }`. `input` is what `drawBracket` takes, or the two finalists with their paths. Options: `team`, `title`, `subtitle`, `final`, `labels`, `groupLabel`, `size`, `winner`, `codes`, `endnote`, and `locale`, `words`, `teamName`, `roundNames`, `markShape`, `flags`, `theme`, `background`, `id`, `className` as for `drawBracket`. The types `RoadChart`, `RoadFinalist`, `RoadMatch`, `RoadOptions` and the rest are exported too; it is also the one export of `/road`.
- `groupStandings(input, options?)`, `groupsTable(input, options?)`: the group stage's tables as data (`GroupTable`, `GroupRow`) and as HTML, from the round-robin games.
- `bracketStyle(options)`: the stylesheet of a drawing with those options and a `styleScope`, for a page that draws a bracket twice. `LAYOUTS` lists the layouts, `CONFERENCE_COLORS` the two half colours of the layouts that colour them (`--tb-conf-a` and `--tb-conf-b`).
- `layoutBracket(input, options?)`: the rounds, halves, rows, byes and results, without drawing. `shape: "ltr"` and `groupOrder` are its options.
- `bracketTable(input, options?)`: the HTML table.
- `describeBracket(input, options?)`: the sentence a screen reader gets.
- `singleElimination(teams, options?)`, `advanceWinners(matchups)`, `standardSeeds(size)`: brackets for any number of teams.
- `matchupsFromGames(games, options)`: matchups from a flat list of games.
- `resolveMatchup`, `collectTeams`, `describeTie`, `noteParts`, `noteLong`: one tie's result, the teams of a bracket, a tie in a sentence.
- `judoOutcome(result, winner?)`, `judoBracket(athletes, options?)`, `judoPodium(input)`, `judoCategoryLabel(category, locale?)`, `JUDO_WEIGHT_CLASSES`, `judoSeconds(time)`, `judoClock(seconds)`: judo results checked against the IJF rules, the bracket with its repechage and two bronze medal contests, the medallists, a weight category as a heading ([docs/judo.md](./judo.md)). `judoScoreboard(contest, options?)`, `describeJudoContest(contest, options?)`, `JUDO_BOARD_SIZES`: the scoreboard of a contest as SVG and as a sentence. `judoContestCard(contest, options?)`, `judoMatQueue(mats, options?)`, `describeJudoCard`, `describeJudoQueue`, `JUDO_MAT_COLORS`: the contest card and the mat queue board as SVG and as sentences. `judoWins(bracket, athlete, options?)`, `judoRoadPanel(source, options?)`, `describeJudoRoad`: an athlete's wins on the road to the final, and the panel. `Matchup.judo` and `Matchup.stage` (`"repechage"`, `"bronze"`) carry them into `drawBracket`.
- `rankingList(source, options?)`, `rankingRanks(entries, options?)`, `rankingCategoryLabel(category, locale?)`, `describeRanking(source, options?)`, `BOARD_SIZES`: a ranking list as SVG, its ranks as data, a category as a heading and the drawing as a sentence. `rankingNumberOnes(source, options?)`, `describeNumberOnes`, `NUMBER_ONE_COLORS`: the number one of each category as cards. `rankingEntryList(source, options?)`, `describeEntryList`: an event's entry list with each athlete's ranking. `rankingMatchup(source, options?)`, `describeMatchup`, `matchupRecord(meetings)`: the head-to-head card of two athletes and their meetings ([docs/rankings.md](./rankings.md)).
- `medalTable(source, options?)`, `medalRanks(rows, options?)`, `tallyMedals(awards, filter?)`, `describeMedalTable(source, options?)`: a medal table as SVG, its ranks as data, a list of medals added up and filtered, and the table as a sentence ([docs/medals.md](./medals.md)).
- `resultsBoard(source, options?)`, `resultsBoardSize(rowCount, options?)`, `describeResultsBoard(source, options?)`, `BROADCAST_LOOKS`: a broadcast-style results board as a standalone SVG, its size from its rows, and the board as a sentence. `boutBoard(source, options?)`, `boutBoardSize(officialCount, options?)`, `describeBoutBoard(source, options?)`: the bout presentation board, its size and its description. `broadcastMedalTable(source, options?)`, `broadcastMedalSize(rowCount, options?)`: the medal table in the broadcast style and its size ([docs/broadcast.md](./broadcast.md)).
- `playoffFormat(league, year)`, `playoffEras(league)`, `LEAGUES`: the formats by era (`nhl`, `nba`, `mlb`, `nfl`, `cfl`, `mls`, `worldcup`). `LEAGUE_EMBLEMS`: each league's default emblem id.
- `isEmblemId(value)`, and the types `EmblemRenderer`, `EmblemRequest`, `EmblemStyle`: the trophy emblems ([marks, flags and trophies](./guides/marks-flags-and-trophies.md)).
- `roundName(remaining)`, `roundNamesFor(count)`, `roundLabel(layout, round, names, words)`: round names.
- `LOCALES`, `LOCALE_CODES`, `defineLocale`, `localeOf`, `voiceOf`, `countryName`: the languages (see Languages), a language of your own, the formats and the names of countries from `Intl`. `localizedLayout(layout, voice)` is a layout with the team names in a language.
- `SIZES`, `THEMES`, `ROAD_THEMES`, `WORDS`, `VERSION`: the sizes (`wide`, `square`, `card`, `phone`), the looks' colours, the road look's extra colours, the English words, the version.
- `BACKGROUND_PRESETS`, `backgroundSvg(preset, options)`, `contrastRatio(a, b)`, `resolveBackground(option, theme, width, height, prefix)`, `FAMILY_BACKGROUNDS_VERSION`: the twenty-five backgrounds, one drawn on its own with the ink that reads over it, the contrast of two colours, the `background` option read, and the version of the file shared with the family ([layouts, looks and backgrounds](./guides/layouts-and-looks.md#backgrounds)). Also its own entry point, `/backgrounds`.
- `cleanSvg(markup)`, `safeUrl(url)`, `safeHref(url)`, `abbreviate(name)`: the rules for marks and for the addresses of links, and the short name made from a name. The types `FlagRenderer`, `FlagRequest` and `FlagShape` are the flags plug-in's ([marks, flags and trophies](./guides/marks-flags-and-trophies.md#flags-from-a-plug-in)).

## Judo: the scoreboard, flip cards and the mat queue

A judo contest is a tie with a `judo` result: ippon, waza-ari, yuko and shido as the IJF's current Sport and Organisation Rules score them (yuko is back since 2025), golden score, the contest time and how it was won. The box writes the marks (`I`, `W`, `Y`), the shido as yellow and red cards and a note under the two rows, in the five languages. `judoBracket` makes a weight category's bracket with the IJF's repechage and its two bronze medal contests. [docs/judo.md](./judo.md) quotes the rules it follows. `judoScoreboard` draws one contest's scoreboard for a given state (the athletes with flags and codes, ippon, waza-ari and yuko counters, shido cards, clock, golden score, osaekomi timer, weight class and round), on a TV or a tablet, as a screen board or as table-top flip cards: [try it in the demo](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/taisen-brackets/#board). `judoContestCard` and `judoMatQueue` draw the card shown when the athletes enter and the board of the next contests per mat, with a big mat number and colour ([demo](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/taisen-brackets/#queue)). `judoWins` and `judoRoadPanel` draw the road to the final of one judoka, the contests they have won on the way, most recent first ([demo](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/taisen-brackets/#judoroad)).

## Rankings, in the style of a provincial federation

`rankingList` draws a ranking for any ranked sport from your own data (a province's or a club's feed, a federation's list), text first, with no photo slot to leave empty: the rank, a flag (a country's or a Canadian province's, `CA-BC`), the name, the club and the points, with the movement since the last list as arrows or row colours. The top three can be big gold, silver and bronze rows above a compact list, a champion row is marked `C`, tied ranks share a number (`10`, `10`), a newly ranked athlete is `NR`, and points are written to the sport's own precision (`91.60`). `rankingNumberOnes` draws the number one of each category as a card (`#1 in -57 kg`), and `rankingEntryList` an event's entry list in the order of the draw with each athlete's ranking beside the name. `rankingMatchup` draws the head-to-head card of two athletes with their previous meetings and the record. `rankingRanks` and `rankingCategoryLabel` are helpers, `describeRanking`, `describeNumberOnes` and `describeEntryList` the descriptions, `NUMBER_ONE_COLORS` the card colours and `BOARD_SIZES` the sizes of every board (a TV, a tablet, a phone, and a square and a 4:5 card for social media). [docs/rankings.md](./rankings.md) · [try it in the demo](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/taisen-brackets/#rankings)

## Broadcast boards

`resultsBoard` draws a results board in the manner of a television graphic: a title band with an icon, a sub bar, and a row for each athlete with a rank in a box, the country code, a 4:3 flag, the name, a record badge (`OR`, `WR`, `NR`) and one or more columns of scores or times at the sport's own precision (a gymnastics final, an all-around, a swimming final, athletics, speed skating). It is one standalone SVG of a width you choose and a height that follows from the rows (`resultsBoardSize`), made to be placed over a picture, in three looks and with no mark of any games or broadcaster. `boutBoard` draws the bout presentation: the two athletes in a red and a blue band, the referee and the judges. `broadcastMedalTable` draws the medal table with a disc for each medal. [docs/broadcast.md](./broadcast.md) · [try it in the demo](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/taisen-brackets/#boards)

## Medal table

`medalTable` draws a medal table for any sport and event (an Olympic Games, a world championship, a club tournament): ranked gold first or by the total, equal rows sharing a rank (5, 5, 7), a flag or a badge for each country or team (a former country such as the Soviet Union is a row of its own), the three medals and the total, in two columns where the board is wide. `tallyMedals` adds up a list of medals filtered to one sport, one weight class or one Games, and `medalRanks` gives the ranks as data. [docs/medals.md](./medals.md) · [try it in the demo](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/taisen-brackets/#medals)
